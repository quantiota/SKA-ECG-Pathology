# VR Library

3D cubes of the learner's space (z, ż, P), to explore on a desktop or in a VR headset.
Each cube shows the transition bands the SKA learner registers from one ECG record.

## Cubes

Sport DB 2.0, athletes at rest, BioHarness 3.0 chest band, 250 Hz. Open a cube and press **Enter VR** in the headset browser.

Four cubes per record, at scale 25: the cube (P), the same with the steps into or out of a missing wave tagged (×), the cube with Δt, the time since the previous wave, as the vertical axis, and the cube with H, the entropy, as the vertical axis. The last column opens P, Δt and H side by side, for a wide display.
